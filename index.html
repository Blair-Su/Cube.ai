Good to know

Before you
begin.

What can I try right now?+

The interactive prototype takes you through Context, Decompose, Ideate, and a 2×2 Map. Write questions, add and arrange ideas, rate your options, and export your board or a direction summary.

Is the AI companion connected?+

Not yet. The current prototype lets you explore the workflow manually. AI conversations and generated suggestions are part of the product vision, and aren’t connected in this version.

What’s the difference between the prototype and demo?+

The prototype starts with a blank workspace for your own challenge. The demo comes with an onboarding brief, three questions, and eight ideas so you can explore a worked example. They are separate websites with separate board data.

Where does my work live?+

Boards are saved in the browser you’re using. Export a board file to keep a copy or move it to another device. Cloud sync and live collaboration aren’t available yet.
